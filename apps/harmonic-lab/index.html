Filter

Your vocal folds make a buzz that contains every harmonic, and your mouth and throat filter it: they boost some frequency regions (the formants) and weaken others. To copy that, keep the source on All harmonics at max, then pick a vowel preset, or capture a vowel and press Fit filter to vowel. Outlined bars show the vowel you are matching and solid bars show what comes out of the filter.
