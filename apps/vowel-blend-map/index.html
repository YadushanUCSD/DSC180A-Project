Vowel Blend Map — place a point, hear the vowel

Each corner is a vowel. Drag the point anywhere inside the shape and the sound is rebuilt from harmonics: the closer you are to a corner, the more of that vowel's formant shape the harmonics take on. Halfway along an edge is exactly halfway between two vowels.

Click or drag to place the point. Hold the mouse down to listen, or press Play to keep it sounding. Small rings mark exact halfway points; the point snaps to them. Space plays and stops; arrow keys nudge the point.
