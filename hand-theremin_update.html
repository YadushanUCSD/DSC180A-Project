Hand Theremin

Right hand: point to set the pitch (left low, up middle, right high) and hold up 1–5 fingers to set the volume. Left hand: each finger you hold up adds one harmonic. A closed left fist gives a pure tone.

How to play

Right hand · pitch & volume
Point
Left is low (110 Hz), up is middle, right is high (1760 Hz)
Fingers
1 quiet → 5 loud, about 6 dB per finger. A fist is silent.
Left hand · harmonics
Fist
Fundamental only, a pure sine tone
Fingers
Each finger adds the next harmonic: 1 adds 2×, 2 adds 3×, up to 6× with 5 fingers
